# KPI Dictionary

## Executive Business Performance Dashboard

This document defines the principal business KPIs used within the Executive Business Performance Dashboard.

The KPI framework was designed to provide senior management with a consistent view of commercial performance, profitability, business activity, growth and performance against plan.

---

## 1. Revenue

**Definition:**  
Total sales revenue generated within the selected reporting context.

**Business Purpose:**  
Provides the primary measure of commercial performance and allows management to assess business scale, growth and performance against revenue targets.

**Calculation:**  
Sum of sales revenue.

**Used For:**
- Executive performance monitoring
- Year-on-year analysis
- Target comparison
- Regional analysis
- Channel analysis
- Product category analysis

---

## 2. Gross Profit

**Definition:**  
Revenue remaining after direct cost of sales.

**Business Purpose:**  
Measures the absolute profitability generated by commercial activity and helps determine whether revenue growth is translating into increased profit.

**Calculation:**

Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost

**Used For:**
- Executive profitability monitoring
- Year-on-year comparison
- Profit target analysis
- Regional profitability analysis
- Channel and category analysis

---

## 3. Gross Margin

**Definition:**  
Gross Profit expressed as a percentage of Revenue.

**Business Purpose:**  
Measures the profitability efficiency of revenue and helps identify situations where sales growth is accompanied by profitability pressure.

**Calculation:**

Gross Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue

**Used For:**
- Profitability monitoring
- Year-on-year margin analysis
- Margin target comparison
- Channel comparison
- Category exception analysis

---

## 4. Revenue YoY

**Definition:**  
Percentage change in Revenue compared with the equivalent prior-year reporting period.

**Business Purpose:**  
Shows whether commercial performance is growing or contracting relative to the previous year.

**Calculation:**

Revenue YoY % = (Current Revenue - Prior Year Revenue) / Prior Year Revenue

**Important Context:**  
The dataset begins in 2023. Therefore, prior-year comparisons are unavailable for 2023 and intentionally return blank rather than zero.

---

## 5. Gross Profit YoY

**Definition:**  
Percentage change in Gross Profit compared with the equivalent prior-year reporting period.

**Business Purpose:**  
Allows management to compare profitability growth with revenue growth.

**Calculation:**

Gross Profit YoY % = (Current Gross Profit - Prior Year Gross Profit) / Prior Year Gross Profit

---

## 6. Gross Margin YoY Change

**Definition:**  
Difference between current Gross Margin and prior-year Gross Margin.

**Business Purpose:**  
Identifies whether profitability efficiency is improving or deteriorating independently of absolute revenue and profit growth.

**Calculation:**

Gross Margin YoY Change = Current Gross Margin - Prior Year Gross Margin

**Unit:**  
Percentage points (pp), not percentage growth.

Example:

27.20% - 27.65% = -0.45 percentage points

---

## 7. Revenue Target

**Definition:**  
Planned Revenue for the selected valid target context.

**Business Purpose:**  
Provides the benchmark required to assess actual commercial performance against plan.

**Supported Target Context:**  
Targets are defined using the supported business dimensions in the target dataset, including time, region and channel.

**Important Modelling Rule:**  
Revenue targets are not allocated artificially to unsupported dimensions such as Product Category.

---

## 8. Revenue vs Target

**Definition:**  
Difference between actual Revenue and planned Revenue.

**Calculation:**

Revenue Variance = Revenue - Revenue Target

**Percentage Calculation:**

Revenue vs Target % = (Revenue - Revenue Target) / Revenue Target

**Business Purpose:**  
Shows whether the business, region or supported commercial segment is ahead of or behind revenue plan.

---

## 9. Profit Target

**Definition:**  
Planned Gross Profit for the selected valid target context.

**Business Purpose:**  
Provides a profitability benchmark independent of Revenue target performance.

---

## 10. Profit vs Target

**Definition:**  
Difference between actual Gross Profit and planned Gross Profit.

**Calculation:**

Profit Variance = Gross Profit - Profit Target

**Percentage Calculation:**

Profit vs Target % = (Gross Profit - Profit Target) / Profit Target

**Business Purpose:**  
Highlights whether profitability is meeting plan and allows comparison between revenue and profit target performance.

---

## 11. Target Gross Margin

**Definition:**  
Gross Margin implied by the Revenue and Gross Profit targets.

**Calculation:**

Target Gross Margin = Profit Target / Revenue Target

**Business Purpose:**  
Provides a profitability-efficiency benchmark against which actual Gross Margin can be compared.

---

## 12. Gross Margin vs Target

**Definition:**  
Difference between actual Gross Margin and Target Gross Margin.

**Calculation:**

Gross Margin vs Target = Actual Gross Margin - Target Gross Margin

**Unit:**  
Percentage points.

---

## 13. Orders

**Definition:**  
Total number of orders within the selected reporting context.

**Business Purpose:**  
Measures transaction activity and provides a commercial driver behind Revenue performance.

---

## 14. Active Customers

**Definition:**  
Distinct customers generating sales activity within the selected reporting context.

**Business Purpose:**  
Shows the size of the active customer base and helps distinguish customer growth from changes in purchasing behaviour.

---

## 15. Average Order Value

**Definition:**  
Average Revenue generated per order.

**Calculation:**

Average Order Value = Revenue / Orders

**Business Purpose:**  
Helps management understand whether Revenue changes are associated with changes in transaction value.

---

## 16. Orders YoY

**Definition:**  
Percentage change in order volume compared with the equivalent prior-year period.

**Business Purpose:**  
Identifies whether commercial activity is expanding or contracting.

---

## 17. Active Customers YoY

**Definition:**  
Percentage change in active customer count compared with the equivalent prior-year period.

**Business Purpose:**  
Shows whether growth is being supported by expansion of the active customer base.

---

## 18. Average Order Value YoY

**Definition:**  
Percentage change in Average Order Value compared with the equivalent prior-year period.

**Business Purpose:**  
Shows whether average transaction value is increasing or decreasing.

---

# Business Classification KPIs

## Growth Quality

**Purpose:**  
Translates combinations of Revenue growth, Gross Profit growth and Gross Margin movement into a management-friendly performance classification.

Example classifications include:

- Healthy Growth
- Growth with Margin Pressure
- Revenue Growth, Profit Decline
- Profit Improvement Despite Revenue Decline
- Business Contraction

This classification is intended to support exception identification rather than replace the underlying financial measures.

---

## Management Attention Status

**Purpose:**  
Provides an exception indicator that helps management identify regions requiring investigation based on defined performance conditions.

Example outputs:

- Attention Required
- Within Tolerance

The status is supported by the underlying KPI values so users can investigate why an area has been flagged.

---

# Target Granularity

A key design principle of the solution is that targets are only compared with actual performance where the target context is valid.

Supported target dimensions include:

- Time
- Region
- Channel

Product Category is not part of the target grain.

Therefore, target-safe measures intentionally return blank when Product Category creates an unsupported comparison context.

This prevents targets from being artificially distributed across categories and avoids presenting misleading target performance.

---

# Reporting Principles

The KPI framework follows four principles:

1. **Actual performance and target performance remain distinguishable.**
2. **Percentage growth and percentage-point movement are not treated as equivalent.**
3. **Unsupported target comparisons return blank rather than fabricated allocations.**
4. **Executive classifications remain traceable to underlying quantitative measures.**
